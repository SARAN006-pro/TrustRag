Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

1. Scheme Overview:
Atal Pension Yojana is a periodic pension scheme targeted at all citizens in the unorganised sector, administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) through the National Pension System (NPS) architecture.

2. Eligibility and Age:
APY is open to all Indian citizens between the ages of 18 and 40 years who have a savings bank account. From 1st October 2022, any citizen who is or has been an income tax payer is not eligible to join APY.

3. Pension Options:
Subscribers receive a guaranteed minimum monthly pension of Rs 1,000, Rs 2,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 4,000, or Rs 5,000 per month upon attaining the age of 60 years, depending on their contribution amount.

4. Monthly Contribution:
Contributions depend on the age of joining and the chosen pension amount. For example, for an entry age of 18 years, the monthly contribution is Rs 42 for a Rs 1,000 pension, and Rs 210 for a Rs 5,000 pension. For an entry age of 40 years, the monthly contribution is Rs 291 for a Rs 1,000 pension and Rs 1,454 for a Rs 5,000 pension. Contributions are auto-debited from the subscriber's savings bank account.

5. Benefits to Spouse and Nominee:
After the death of the subscriber, the same monthly pension amount is payable to the spouse for life. Upon the death of both the subscriber and spouse, the accumulated pension wealth (corpus up to Rs 8.5 lakh for Rs 5,000 pension) is returned to the nominee.
