Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

1. Purpose and Coverage:
PMSBY is an accident insurance scheme offering coverage against accidental death and permanent disability. The scheme provides accidental death and full disability cover of Rs 2,00,000, and partial permanent disability cover of Rs 1,00,000.

2. Eligibility:
Citizens between 18 and 70 years of age with an active savings bank account or post office account are eligible. The coverage period runs from 1st June to 31st May each year.

3. Premium:
The annual premium is Rs 20 per annum per subscriber, which is auto-debited in a single instalment annually from the account holder's savings bank account.

4. Exclusions and Termination:
Accidents arising from self-inflicted injury, suicide, or war are excluded. Coverage terminates when the member reaches 70 years of age, or if the account is closed or has insufficient balance to pay the annual premium.
