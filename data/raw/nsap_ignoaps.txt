Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS - NSAP)

1. Scheme Purpose:
IGNOAPS is a non-contributory component of the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) administered by the Ministry of Rural Development, providing monthly social pensions to senior citizens living below the poverty line.

2. Eligibility:
(a) The applicant must be 60 years of age or older.
(b) The applicant must belong to a household living below the poverty line (BPL) according to the criteria prescribed by the Government of India.

3. Central Assistance Amount:
- For persons between 60 and 79 years of age, the Central Government provides assistance of Rs 200 per month.
- For persons who are 80 years of age and above, the Central Government assistance is Rs 500 per month.
State Governments are encouraged to supplement the central pension with equal or higher state contributions, resulting in total monthly pensions ranging between Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,500 depending on the state.

4. Mode of Payment:
Pension is disbursed monthly directly into the beneficiary's post office or bank savings account, or via electronic benefit transfer. In remote areas, disbursement is allowed through cash at Panchayat offices.
