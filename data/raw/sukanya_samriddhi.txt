Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

1. Purpose:
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a small deposit scheme by the Government of India under the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign, aimed at meeting the education and marriage expenses of girl children.

2. Opening of Account:
An account can be opened by the natural or legal guardian in the name of a girl child from the birth of the girl child until she attains the age of 10 years. A family can open only one account per girl child and maximum two accounts for two girl children, except in the case of twin or triplet girls born in the first or second order.

3. Deposit Limits:
The account can be opened with a minimum initial deposit of Rs 250. Subsequent deposits must be in multiples of Rs 50. The minimum deposit in a financial year is Rs 250 and the maximum limit is Rs 1,50,000 per financial year. Deposits can be made for a maximum period of 15 years from the date of opening of the account.

4. Interest Rate and Tax Benefit:
The interest rate is determined by the Government on a quarterly basis. Investments qualify for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act up to Rs 1.5 lakh. The interest accrued and the maturity proceeds are completely tax-free (EEE status).

5. Maturity and Partial Withdrawal:
The account matures after 21 years from the date of its opening or upon the marriage of the girl child after attaining 18 years of age. Partial withdrawal of up to 50% of the balance standing at the end of the preceding financial year is allowed for higher education once the girl child reaches 18 years or passes Class 10.
