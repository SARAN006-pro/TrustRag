Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM)

1. Scheme Purpose:
PM-SYM is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme for unorganised workers for ensuring old age protection. It is administered by the Ministry of Labour and Employment and implemented through Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) and Common Services Centres (CSCs).

2. Eligibility Criteria:
(a) The applicant must be an unorganised worker (such as street vendors, head loaders, brick kiln workers, cobblers, rag pickers, domestic workers, rickshaw pullers, landless labourers, agricultural workers, construction workers).
(b) Entry age must be between 18 and 40 years.
(c) Monthly income should be Rs 15,000 or below.
(d) Should not be an engaged member of EPFO, ESIC, or NPS (Government funded).
(e) Should not be an income tax assessee.

3. Monthly Pension Benefit:
Each subscriber under PM-SYM receives a minimum assured monthly pension of Rs 3,000 once they attain the age of 60 years.

4. Contribution Pattern:
PM-SYM operates on a 50:50 matching basis. The subscriber contributes an age-specific monthly amount ranging from Rs 55 (at entry age 18) to Rs 200 (at entry age 40). An equal 50% matching contribution is paid by the Central Government into the subscriber's pension account.

5. Family Pension:
During the receipt of pension, if the subscriber dies, the spouse is entitled to receive 50% of the pension as family pension (Rs 1,500 per month). Family pension is applicable only to the spouse.

6. Required Documents:
To enroll, the individual needs an Aadhaar card and a savings bank account or Jan Dhan account with IFSC code.
