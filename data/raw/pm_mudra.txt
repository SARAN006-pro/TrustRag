Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

1. Purpose and Scope:
Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) was launched on 8th April 2015 to provide collateral-free institutional credit up to Rs 10 lakh to micro and small non-farm, non-corporate enterprises for income-generating activities in manufacturing, processing, trading, and services.

2. Loan Categories:
MUDRA loans are classified into three distinct categories reflecting the stage of development and funding needs of the beneficiary micro-unit:
- Shishu: covering loans up to Rs 50,000 for entrepreneurs starting business or needing seed finance.
- Kishore: covering loans above Rs 50,000 and up to Rs 5,00,000 for enterprise consolidation and scaling.
- Tarun: covering loans above Rs 5,00,000 and up to Rs 10,00,000 for mature small enterprises requiring business expansion.

3. Security and Collateral:
No collateral or third-party guarantee is required for MUDRA loans up to Rs 10 lakh. Primary security consists of hypothecation of assets created through the bank loan.

4. Lending Institutions:
Loans under PMMY are delivered by Public Sector Banks, Private Sector Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Small Finance Banks (SFBs), Micro Finance Institutions (MFIs), and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

5. Repayment Tenure:
The repayment tenure is generally between 3 to 5 years, including a suitable moratorium period depending on cash flows.
