Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

1. Scheme Objective:
Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin aims to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to all houseless households and those households living in kutcha and dilapidated houses in rural areas.

2. Financial Assistance:
Under PMAY-G, the unit assistance provided is Rs 1,20,000 in plain areas and Rs 1,30,000 in hilly states, difficult areas, and Integrated Action Plan (IAP) districts. The cost of house construction is shared between the Central Government and State Governments in the ratio 60:40 in plain areas and 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan States.

3. Minimum House Size:
The minimum unit size of the house constructed under PMAY-G is 25 square metres including a dedicated clean cooking space.

4. Supplementary Support:
Beneficiaries are entitled to 90 to 95 person-days of unskilled labour wage under Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS) for house construction. In addition, assistance of Rs 12,000 is provided for the construction of an individual household latrine (IHHL) under Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin (SBM-G) or any other dedicated scheme.

5. Identification of Beneficiaries:
The identification and selection of beneficiaries under PMAY-G is done through Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data, finalized by the Gram Sabha. Families that own motorized two-wheelers, mechanized three or four-wheelers, motorized fishing boats, or mechanized agricultural equipment are excluded. Households with a member earning more than Rs 10,000 per month or paying income tax are also excluded.
