PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)

1. Background and Objective:
PM SVANidhi is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to facilitate working capital loans to street vendors to resume their livelihoods affected by the COVID-19 pandemic.

2. Loan Tranches:
The scheme provides collateral-free working capital loans in three progressive tranches:
- First tranche: loan up to Rs 10,000 with a repayment tenure of 1 year.
- Second tranche: upon timely or early repayment of the first loan, eligible for a loan up to Rs 20,000 with tenure of up to 18 months.
- Third tranche: on timely repayment of the second loan, eligible for a loan up to Rs 50,000 with tenure of up to 36 months.

3. Interest Subsidy:
An interest subsidy at the rate of 7% per annum is credited directly into the bank accounts of vendors on a quarterly basis upon timely repayment of loans. No pre-payment penalty is charged for early repayment.

4. Digital Incentives:
The scheme promotes digital transactions through cashback incentives of up to Rs 100 per month (Rs 1,200 per year) for eligible digital receipts.

5. Eligibility:
Street vendors and hawkers vending in urban areas as on or before March 24, 2020 are eligible. Vendors must possess a Certificate of Vending or Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), or a Letter of Recommendation (LoR).

6. Documents Required:
Aadhaar Card or Voter Identity Card, Certificate of Vending or Letter of Recommendation from ULB, and an active bank account.
