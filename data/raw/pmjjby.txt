Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

1. Purpose and Coverage:
PMJJBY is a government-backed life insurance scheme providing life cover for death due to any reason. The life insurance cover provided is Rs 2,00,000.

2. Eligibility:
Individuals aged between 18 and 50 years having a savings bank account or a post office bank account are eligible to join the scheme. The cover terminates when the subscriber reaches 55 years of age.

3. Premium:
The premium is Rs 436 per annum per member, which is auto-debited from the subscriber's bank account in a single instalment on or before 31st May each year for the annual coverage period from 1st June to 31st May.

4. Lien Period:
A lien period of 30 days is applicable from the date of enrollment. During the first 30 days of coverage, death due to reasons other than accident is not covered. Accidental deaths are covered from day one.

5. Claim Process:
In the event of the insured person's death, the nominee receives Rs 2 lakh upon submitting the death certificate, claim form, and bank account details.
