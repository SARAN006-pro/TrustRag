Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

1. Overview and Coverage:
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest health assurance scheme fully financed by the Government. It aims to provide a health cover of Rs 5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization to over 12 crore poor and vulnerable families (approximately 55 crore beneficiaries).

2. Benefit Package:
The benefit cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year is available on a family floater basis, which means that can be used by one or all members of the family. There is no restriction on family size, age, or gender of family members. Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one of the policy.

3. Scope of Services:
PM-JAY covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization expenses and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses such as diagnostics, medicines, and medical care. The services include approximately 1,949 treatment procedures across 27 specialties. Treatment is completely cashless and paperless at public and empanelled private hospitals across India.

4. Eligibility:
Eligible households are identified based on the deprivation and occupational criteria of the Socio-Economic and Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011) for rural and urban areas respectively. There is no enrollment process; families whose names are in the SECC database or National Food Security Act (NFSA) eligible lists are automatically entitled. An Ayushman card is generated to facilitate cashless treatment.

5. Exclusions:
Families in rural areas that own motorized vehicles, motorized agricultural machinery, or Kisan Credit Cards with credit limit of Rs 50,000 or more are excluded. Government employees, households paying income tax, or households with non-agricultural enterprises registered with government are not eligible.
