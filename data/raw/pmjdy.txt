Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

1. Objectives:
PMJDY is a National Mission for Financial Inclusion to ensure access to financial services, namely banking/savings and deposit accounts, remittance, credit, insurance, and pension in an affordable manner.

2. Zero Balance and Interest:
Accounts can be opened with zero balance. Account holders earn interest on deposits made in PMJDY accounts.

3. RuPay Card and Inbuilt Insurance:
Every account holder is provided a RuPay Debit Card. For accounts opened after 28th August 2018, the RuPay card comes with free inbuilt accidental insurance cover of Rs 2,00,000 (previously Rs 1,00,000), provided the card has been used at least once in 90 days.

4. Overdraft Facility:
An overdraft facility of up to Rs 10,000 is available to eligible account holders after satisfactory operation of the account for 6 months. For overdraft up to Rs 2,000, no conditions apply. The age limit for availing overdraft is 18 to 65 years. Only one overdraft facility is allowed per household, preferably to the lady of the house.

5. Direct Benefit Transfer:
PMJDY accounts serve as the primary conduit for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of all government welfare subsidies under schemes like PM-KISAN, PAHAL (LPG subsidy), and MGNREGA.
